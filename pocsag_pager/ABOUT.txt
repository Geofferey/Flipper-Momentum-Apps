Receive POCSAG and FLEX pager messages, or send an alphanumeric POCSAG page to your own node.
Default frequency is set to
DAPNET - 439987500

Select POCSAG (POCSAG-W at 900 MHz) for POCSAG, FLEX for 2-level FLEX and a FLEX4 preset for 4-level. RSSI and hopping are disabled with FLEX4.

Frequencies are in
/pocsag/settings.txt
Custom frequencies are at the end of the list.

Supported protocols:
POCSAG 512
POCSAG 1200
POCSAG 2400
FLEX 1600/2
FLEX 3200/2
FLEX 3200/4
FLEX 6400/4
